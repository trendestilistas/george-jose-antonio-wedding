02 / What to wear

Dress to Celebrate

✦

We're celebrating in a castle, so why not make the most of the occasion?

Our dress code is cocktail attire — suits for gentlemen and cocktail or elegant day dresses for ladies.

Hats, fascinators and elegant hair accessories are very welcome, and if you fancy dressing a little extra to impress, we'd absolutely love that! A touch of glamour, a little individuality and some fabulous outfits are all encouraged.

We kindly ask guests to avoid full white, cream, beige or red outfits, although accents and accessories in these colours are absolutely welcome.

To complement the occasion, we also ask that trainers and sneakers are left at home.

Above all, wear something that makes you feel wonderful, confident and ready to celebrate in style!

05 / The best of both cultures

A Little Spanish Flair

✦

With our friends and family coming together from different countries and cultures, we're bringing a few Spanish wedding traditions to our celebration.

There is no “George's side” or “Jose Antonio's side”. We're one big celebration — sit wherever you feel comfortable, meet someone new and enjoy the day together.

¡Que vivan los novios!
“Long live the grooms!”

You'll probably hear this shouted throughout the day. Don't be shy — the traditional response is a very enthusiastic...

¡Viva!

Whether you're joining us from England, Spain or somewhere else entirely, we can't wait to celebrate together.

06 / With love

Wedding Gifts

Your presence is our present

Having you with us to celebrate our wedding means more than any gift ever could.

We know that joining us may involve travel, accommodation and other expenses, and we genuinely don't expect presents. Your presence, your love and the memories we'll make together are more than enough.

For anyone who would still like to give a little something, it's customary in Spain to give a monetary wedding gift rather than choose from a traditional gift list.

Any contribution would be gratefully received and put towards our wedding celebrations, honeymoon and the next chapter of our lives together.

Please don't feel any obligation whatsoever — celebrating with you is what matters most.

07 / Helpful details

Good to Know

A few answers to help you enjoy the day without a worry.

What time should I arrive?

Guest arrival is planned from 1pm. Our ceremony begins at 2pm, so please arrive in good time. We'll confirm any further arrival guidance closer to the wedding.

Can I bring a plus-one?

Our invitations are for named guests only, so please RSVP only for the people included in your invitation.

What should I wear?

We're celebrating in cocktail attire: suits for gentlemen and cocktail or elegant day dresses for ladies. Hats, fascinators and glamorous accessories are welcome! Please avoid full white, cream, beige or red outfits (small accents are fine), and no trainers or sneakers.

How do I request a room?

When the combined RSVP and accommodation form opens, you'll be able to request a one- or two-night package. Requests will be timestamped and considered in the order received, subject to availability. Please don't treat your request as a confirmed booking until we contact you.

How will I pay for accommodation?

We'll contact you personally to confirm room availability, the final price and your individual payment link. No accommodation payments are being collected through this preview website.

What about the bar?

We'll be treating everyone to drinks at the start of the celebrations. Once our hosted bar tab has been used, further drinks will be available to purchase by card only.

Will there be food during the wedding?

Yes. We'll be enjoying a traditional sit-down wedding breakfast and evening food on the wedding day. Both accommodation packages include these meals.

Can I travel on a different flight?

Absolutely. You're welcome to make your own travel arrangements, but the shared return coach will only serve the suggested flight arrangements. Other flights will require your own transfer to and from the venue.

Is there parking at the castle?

We'll share confirmed parking and arrival details here closer to the wedding. In the meantime, please get in touch if you have a specific requirement.

Do we need to bring a gift?

Not at all. We know guests are making an effort to join us and your presence truly is enough. If you'd still like to give something, please see the Wedding Gifts section.
